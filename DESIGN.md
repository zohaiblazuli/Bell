# Bell design system

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## World

Bell App v2 is drawn in the **Shape Kit**: a cream study desk ruled in ink, built from a handful of
flat geometric shapes — circle, quarter, triangle, square, half, diamond — in four colours. Nothing is
glass, nothing blurs, nothing is rounded unless it is a real circle. The owl-head mark (blue head,
black ears) and the "Bell." wordmark with its blue full stop are the brand; **Hush**, the owl built
from the same shapes, is the only mascot. Actual documents stay bright and visually dominant.

## Foundations

- Tokens are generated by `scripts/tokens.mjs` into `src/styles/tokens.css`: Day on `:root`, Night
  (warm charcoal `#262624`) on `.app[data-tone='night']`. Do not introduce feature-local palettes.
- Palette: ink `#1A1A1A`, blue `#2A5CAA`, red `#D9432F`, yellow `#F2B632` on cream. Tone-paired
  tokens (`--ink`, `--line`, `--blue`, `--red`, `--pale`, `--pale2`, `--mute`, tints `--t-*`) follow
  Day/Night; `--sk-*` literals do not, and are for objects that keep their colour in the dark (the
  logo, Hush, notebook covers, reaction faces).
- Type: **Jost** is the interface voice, **Azeret Mono** carries codes, counts, eyebrows and dates,
  **Nanum Pen Script** is handwriting. All three are vendored in `src/assets/fonts`.
- Frames are 2px ink rules (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 2px var(--line)`); raised things (dialogs,
  menus, the palette, the update corner) add a hard offset ink shadow — 6px or 8px, never blurred.
- Selection inverts: the chosen row, chip or segment becomes ink with cream text. Blue marks focus and
  progress; red is for the live moment (today, the current page, errors, the ringing bell).
- Paper numbers have shapes: P1 red circle, P2 blue quarter, P3 blue triangle, P4 yellow square, P5
  ink half, P6 red diamond (`ui/shapekit/PaperShape.tsx`).

## Geometry and rhythm

- Every radius token is 0. Circles use `50%`; the kit's quarter and half shapes are the only other
  curves.
- The window is a 228px sidebar and a stage. Top bars are 56px on a 2px rule. Document tabs appear
  only inside document views, as a 40px row under the Reader's own bar.
- Sections sit on a mono eyebrow and a 2px rule rather than in boxes; only the thing to act on next
  (the Home resume hero, a dialog) gets a full frame.

## Motion

- All keyframes live in `src/styles/motion.css`, prefixed `sk-`. Every animated element rests in its
  final state, so reduced motion (the OS setting or Settings → Reduce motion) simply shows the end
  frame.
- Views iris in from the top-left; cards are dealt; shapes roll, swing and pop into place; the bell
  quarter rings. The Day/Night switch is a circle reveal from the pressed sun or moon (View
  Transitions API).
- Startup v2, "Full stop": the logo builds straight on the desktop, then the window opens behind it
  and the lockup flies into the sidebar.
- View-level entrance animations must not fill forwards on `opacity`, `transform` or `clip-path`:
  a lingering stacking context or clip traps modal scrims raised inside the view.

## Community Resources direction

The chosen direction is a preview-led **paper desk** (Operate seed `135520f6`, assigned structure 5).
The resource itself earns trust, so the first-page preview owns the largest share of the catalogue
viewport. A compact resource rail narrows the collection on the left; provenance, file facts, trust
state, upvote, and the local-download action sit beside the selected paper. This is a study catalogue,
not a feed, marketplace, or promotional card wall.

The opening-frame contract is:

- Search and compact qualification/subject/type/sort controls span the top.
- The resource rail is subordinate to a large selected-paper preview.
- Authorship, uploader identity, file facts, and approval language stay visible with the preview.
- Administration replaces the desk only after privileged authentication and uses a compact
  operational table, explicit scan/IP/audit inspection, and one upload form.
- The reader reuses Bell's existing paper canvas and annotation tools so community documents do not
  feel like a separate product.
- Reactions are the kit's four faces (Helpful, Wow, Clear, Confusing), stored on this machine only;
  the desk shows no reaction counts.

## Quality bar

Aim for the clarity and density of a well-made native document library: strong frame hierarchy,
paper-led depth, flat shapes instead of ornament, crisp metadata lettering, complete keyboard/focus states, and
no clipped controls at Bell's 1040×680 minimum window. Delight comes from the Bell world and the
document-stage composition, not extra decoration.

## Responsive and accessibility rules

- At narrower desktop widths, compress the rail and metadata pane before reducing legibility; wrap
  actions and keep every control reachable without horizontal page scrolling.
- Focus is visible on every interactive element. Status uses text in addition to colour. Dialogs
  expose modal semantics, take initial focus, and close with Escape when no operation is active.
- Respect reduced motion and retain Day/Night contrast through semantic tokens.
